## Part B — Data we process for our customers

When an organization uses ARCH to run incidents, the organization decides what content goes in, and
which of its people get access. **The organization is the data controller (or "data fiduciary"); ARCH
is the data processor (or "data processor").**

What this means in practice:

1. **We process customer content only to provide the Service** — storing incidents, rendering status
   pages, sending notifications the organization configured, keeping audit logs.
2. **We do not mine, sell or reuse customer content.** We do not train AI models on it — ARCH has no
   AI features in v1.
3. **Support access is limited and logged.** ARCH staff view customer content only with the customer's
   permission for a specific support case (or where legally compelled). Access is logged in our audit
   trail.
4. **Data subject requests** concerning content inside a customer organization are handled by that
   organization; we assist with the technical means (export, deletion, correction) described in the
   [DPA](DATA-PROCESSING-ADDENDUM.md).
5. **Instructions** are the Terms of Service, the DPA, and the features you choose to use. If we
   believe an instruction violates law, we will tell you and may decline it.

If you are an individual inside a customer organization, we cannot delete or export your data on your
own instruction — please contact your organization's administrators first. We will help them respond.
