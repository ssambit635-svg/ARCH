# Contributor Code of Conduct

**Version:** 1.0 · **Applies to:** everyone in the ARCH repository, issue tracker and project channels

---

## Our pledge

We want a project where the hardest thing is the engineering. Everyone who participates — regardless
of experience level, age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, nationality,
personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation — should be able to do their
best work without being belittled, harassed or exhausted by the people around them.

We also care about what we build: an **incident management tool**, used by people under pressure at bad
hours. Blamelessness is not a slogan here, it is the working method. The same principle applies to code
review, documentation feedback and disagreement about design.

---

## Our standards

**Behaviour that strengthens the project:**

- **Technical criticism, not personal criticism.** "This query will do a full table scan" is help.
  "This is amateur work" is not.
- **Assume the other person has context you don't.** Especially in an incident-adjacent project, where
  the author may have shipped at 3 a.m. under pressure.
- **Say "I don't know"** and ask a question. Nobody is expected to already know the answer.
- **Credit other people's work**, in code comments, changelogs, issues and docs.
- **Blameless post-mortems.** When reviewing a mistake, ask what made it easy to make. Fix the system,
  not the person.
- **Respect confidentiality.** Customer data, security reports and incident content are not gossip.
- **Accept a decision you argued against**, once it is made — or reopen it with new evidence, calmly.

**Behaviour that is unacceptable:**

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language, imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Trolling, deliberate derailing, or repeated bad-faith argument.
- Publishing someone's private information (email, address, employer) without permission.
- Using ARCH's channels to promote unrelated products or services.
- Dismissing a report of a security or safety issue in order to close a discussion.
- Retaliating against anyone who reports a concern in good faith.

---

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces — repository, issues, pull requests, documentation, project
chat — and when an individual is representing the project in public, including on social media and at
events.

---

## Reporting

Report concerns to **[CONDUCT EMAIL]**. Reports are read by the project maintainers and are treated as
confidential; details are shared only with those who need to know to resolve the issue, or where
required by law.

Include what happened, where, when, and who was involved if you are comfortable doing so. If you are
not comfortable naming names, report it anyway — a pattern often becomes visible from partial reports.

**What you can expect:**
- Acknowledgement within **72 hours**.
- An assessment and a decision within **14 days**.
- No requirement to resolve it with the other person directly.
- Protection from retaliation. Retaliation is itself a serious violation.

---

## Enforcement

The response depends on the severity and pattern, not on who the person is or how much they contribute:

| Level | Behaviour | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Correction** | Unprofessional or dismissive behaviour | Private written warning naming the behaviour and its impact; a public apology may be requested |
| **2. Warning** | A single violation, or a repeated pattern | Warning with conditions on interaction for a stated period; no unprompted contact with the affected person |
| **3. Temporary ban** | A serious violation, or continuing after a warning | Temporary exclusion from all project spaces for a stated period |
| **4. Permanent ban** | A pattern of violations, harassment, or violence or threats | Permanent exclusion from the project |

The project maintainers apply these consistently. They recuse themselves from any decision about
conduct they are personally involved in, and where a maintainer is a party to a report, an uninvolved
maintainer decides.
